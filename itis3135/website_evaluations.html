Website Evaluations

These websites are being evaluated for the CRAP web design princples: Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity.

Apple

  • Contrast: Apple uses strong contrast to make important content stand out. Large headings, product images, and buttons are easy to distinguish from the background, creating a clear visual hierarchy.
  • Repetition: Apple consistently repeats its typography, navigation, button styles, spacing, and overall visual style throughout the website. This creates a cohesive appearance and makes the site feel consistent.
  • Alignment: Apple's content is organized using clean and consistent alignment. Text, images, buttons, and other elements are positioned in an orderly way, making the pages easy to scan and navigate.
  • Proximity: Related information is grouped closely together. Product images, descriptions, headings, and buttons are positioned near one another so it is clear which pieces of information belong together.

Craigslist

  • Contrast: Craigslist uses relatively little visual contrast. Much of the page consists of similarly sized text links, making it difficult to immediately identify the most important information or sections of the page.
  • Repetition: Craigslist does use repetition through its consistent text link format and simple page structure. However, the repeated presentation of information can make the page feel monotonous and makes different categories difficult to visually distinguish.
  • Alignment: The website contains many columns and lists of links without a strong modern visual grid. Although the information is technically organized, the large amount of text creates a cluttered appearance and makes the page harder to scan.
  • Proximity: Craigslist places many categories and subcategories close together. Because there is so much information packed into a relatively small area, it can be difficult to quickly distinguish between different groups of content.
